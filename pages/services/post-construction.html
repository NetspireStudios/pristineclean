Our Expertise

Cleaning That
Goes Beyond the Basics

Post-construction cleaning is very different from regular housekeeping. Building materials leave behind fine dust that can settle inside vents, on baseboards, and even inside cupboards. Adhesives, paint drips, grout haze, and drywall residue can cling to surfaces that need special care. This type of work requires time, proper tools, and a careful approach.

Our cleaners follow a slow, methodical process that tackles every layer of construction debris. We start with debris removal and surface dusting, followed by careful vacuuming using equipment designed to capture fine particles. From there, we wipe down walls, trim, corners, doors, outlets, and fixtures. Floors are cleaned based on their material, whether tile, hardwood, laminate, or carpet.

If glass, mirrors, or stainless steel appliances were installed, we clean those surfaces until they are free of fingerprints, smudges, and installation residue. The goal is to leave the space clean, safe, and comfortable for day-to-day living.

Detailed Care

Attention to Detail
for Renovations of Any Size

Every renovation leaves its own challenges. A kitchen remodel may leave behind heavy dust inside drawers or around appliance openings. A basement renovation often creates dust that travels up staircases and into nearby rooms. Bathroom remodels sometimes leave grout haze on tiles or fine dust on newly installed fixtures.

Our post-construction cleaning service takes these details into account. We clean rooms individually and then move outward through the home so nothing is re-contaminated. This approach helps us leave your property looking fresh and ready to use. It also allows contractors and homeowners to complete their projects with confidence, knowing that the final impression will match the quality of the renovation.
